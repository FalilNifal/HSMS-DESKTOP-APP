\# HSMS Desktop App - Feature Plan



\## Project Goal



HSMS Desktop App is a Windows-based offline hardware store management system. The app will support product management, supplier management, POS billing, invoice printing, profit tracking, role-based users, and backup/restore.



\## Target Platform



\- Windows desktop

\- Single-computer usage

\- Fully offline

\- Local database

\- Installable as a desktop application



\## Main Modules



\### 1. Authentication and Setup



\- First-time setup wizard

\- First admin account creation

\- Login system

\- Role-based access control

\- Admin, Manager, and Cashier roles

\- Manager and Cashier can change their own password

\- Admin can create and manage users



\### 2. Product and Inventory Management



\- Add products

\- Edit products

\- Delete/deactivate products

\- Search products

\- Manage stock quantity

\- Low-stock alerts

\- Supplier link

\- Category support



\### 3. Pricing and Profit Tracking



Each product should have:



\- Purchase Price

\- Minimum Selling Price

\- Current Stock Quantity



During billing:



\- User can enter custom selling price

\- Selling price cannot be lower than minimum selling price

\- Profit is calculated using purchase price



Formula:



Profit = (Actual Selling Price - Purchase Price) × Quantity



\### 4. Sales and Billing



\- Fast POS billing screen

\- Add products to cart

\- Change quantity

\- Enter selling price

\- Validate selling price

\- Create sale

\- Reduce stock after sale

\- Generate invoice

\- Print invoice/bill



\### 5. Reports



\- Daily sales report

\- Monthly sales report

\- Profit report

\- Product-wise sales

\- Low-stock report

\- Cashier-wise sales

\- Export reports



\### 6. Backup and Restore



\- Manual backup

\- Restore from backup

\- Automatic daily backup

\- Backup database and shop settings



\### 7. Shop Settings



\- Shop name

\- Address

\- Phone number

\- Logo

\- Invoice footer message

\- Currency

\- Printer settings
